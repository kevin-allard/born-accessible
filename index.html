For product evaluation

Accessibility Conformance Reports

An Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR) helps you understand a product’s accessibility features and known limitations, with details about the version and experience evaluated.

ACRs are available upon request through the Accessibility Team. The team can help identify the appropriate report and explain its scope.

For the classroom

Accessible Pathways

For older products with accessibility limitations, Accessible Pathways provide guidance to help teachers and districts support equitable participation. Depending on the program and learner’s needs, this may include alternative ways to access content or educator-supported activities.

Access this guidance through our Customer Support network. Available pathways and materials vary by program.

Accessible Pathways address classroom participation needs. For details about a product’s accessibility features and limitations, our Accessibility Team can help you review the relevant ACR.

Your accessibility questions.

What does Born Accessible mean for the products I use?

Born Accessible guides the development of our next-generation learning experiences. Accessibility features vary across existing products, and some legacy programs have known limitations. Our Accessibility Team can help you understand your product’s ACR, while Customer Support can connect you with available Accessible Pathways.

How does accessibility shape new content?

The approach begins with structured content and accessible design choices at the source. Priorities include meaningful text alternatives, navigable reading structures, accessible mathematical expressions, and experiences that support assistive technology. Our Accessibility Team can help you explore the capabilities available in your product and version.

What happens when I report a barrier?

Customer Support gathers the product, activity, and access issue so the appropriate team can review it. The team considers available instructional pathways and needed improvements. Program-specific content issues are considered as titles come up for revision. Customer Support can help you explore available guidance for your current program.

Our Commitments / Responsible AI

Human purpose.
Responsible AI.

Technology should strengthen the people at the heart of learning.

Our approach to AI centers on educators, learners, and human judgment. We are committed to meaningful instructional value and thoughtful oversight of how AI is developed, evaluated, and used.

Principles that guide
responsible innovation.

Our AI principles put learning first, with educator agency, transparency, fairness, and responsible stewardship at the center.

01 / People & purpose

Keep educators in control.

We believe AI serves learning best when educators remain in control. Human expertise is central to instructional decisions, interpretation, and the review of AI-assisted work.

02 / Transparency

Make AI understandable.

We are committed to helping educators understand where AI is involved, the purpose it serves, and its limitations. Clear information supports informed judgment when reviewing AI-assisted content and recommendations.

03 / Fairness & accessibility

Design for diverse learners.

Our commitment to responsible AI includes attention to bias, representation, and accessibility. Diverse learners and classroom contexts inform how we evaluate and improve learning experiences.

04 / Stewardship

Respect data and creative work.

Responsible innovation includes respect for personal information and creative work. Our approach brings privacy, security, data use, and intellectual property considerations into AI review alongside educational purpose.
